\section{Results: Quality}
\label{sec:results-quality}

This section reports the quality axis of the study on the retrieval-QA
parity benchmarks in scope for this preprint (C1): how much
cross-document attention can be removed and how the reported single-run
quality estimates change.

\subsection{Train-free mask sweep}
\label{sec:results-quality-sweep}

The primary quality result is the train-free sweep: for every combination
of primary model, base/instruct variant, dataset, and mask variant, the
mask is swapped at inference on stock weights. The available artifacts do
not provide a uniform fixed-set, multi-checkpoint replication protocol for
the displayed comparisons, so the non-baseline cells below are descriptive
single-run estimates rather than inferential comparisons.

Table~\ref{tab:t2} reports train-free quality on the available model
families and datasets. These single-run rows characterize the listed cells;
they do not establish a model-family-wide property of the mask.

\paragraph{Matched three-seed transfer study.}
The transfer protocol trains three top-8-layer \texttt{ddi\_qf} checkpoints
per architecture and evaluates base and tuned \texttt{full},
\texttt{ddi\_qf}, and \texttt{blind} arms on seed-specific frozen manifests.
Each natural-QA manifest contains 100 records. Within each architecture, the
preregistered statistic $C=1-(\mathrm{tuned\ full}-\mathrm{tuned\ ddi})/(\mathrm{base\ full}-\mathrm{base\ blind})$
is retained unchanged for its historical decision role. It is a base-headroom-normalized
\emph{remaining tuned gap}, not a fraction recovered by training. Both Qwen and Llama fail the
preregistered parity gate on musique F1, with mean closure 0.452 and 0.480,
respectively. Hotpot F1 is also below parity for Qwen, while Llama hotpot is
invalidated by movement of the blind control. Both EM cross-architecture
contrasts are unavailable because at least one architecture fails
admissibility or control stability.

Table~\ref{tab:t7-transfer} reports the cross-architecture decision. The only
valid natural-QA contrast is musique F1: Llama minus Qwen closure is 0.028,
with a 95\% equal-seed bootstrap interval of $[-0.085, 0.179]$. The point
estimate is below the preregistered 0.10-headroom practical threshold and the
interval crosses zero. Thus no practical architecture difference is
established; this is not evidence that the architectures are equivalent.

Table~\ref{tab:t8-transfer-estimands} separates the training estimands. Positive
$\Delta$ DDI is a paired base-to-tuned DDI improvement, positive $\Delta$ full
is full-path improvement, and negative $\Delta$ gap narrows the full--DDI gap.
The table does not replace $C$ or its preregistered gates. The post-hoc paired
example-resampling intervals are conditional on the retained checkpoints and
fixed records, rather than population confidence intervals.

Figure~\ref{fig:transfer-estimands} visualizes why the historical scalar $C$
must not be read as ``training recovery.'' DDI scores can improve while the
full path moves downward; the change in the full--DDI gap is therefore a
combination of both effects. The bars are paired changes from the retained
three-seed study, not independent confidence intervals.

\paragraph{Cross-family transfer is not free.}
Applying the identical 8-layer sequential distillation recipe to
Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (13.1 GPU-hours, 26{,}217 steps) does \emph{not}
close the gap: hotpot EM stays at $+0.16$ (0.47 vs 0.31) and musique at
$+0.18$ (0.33 vs 0.15). The student perplexity plateaus at 1.840 against
a teacher of 1.580, where the same recipe on Qwen converged to within
0.007 of the teacher (1.453 vs 1.446). The distillation recipe is
architecture-sensitive --- the parity claim (C1) is currently supported
on Qwen2.5-7B only, and closing the gap on Llama is open.

\subsection{Finetuned quality}
\label{sec:results-quality-finetune}

The train-free sweep shows a quality gap between \texttt{ddi\_qf} and
\texttt{full} (Table~\ref{tab:t2}): on hotpot\_qa, EM drops from 0.57
(full) to 0.31 (ddi\_qf). We attempt to reduce this gap via self-distillation: the
student model runs under \texttt{ddi\_qf} and is trained to match the
teacher's answer-position logits under \texttt{full}, using KL divergence
on the answer tokens. Only the top 8 of 28 decoder layers are trainable
(1.86B parameters); the rest are frozen.

Table~\ref{tab:t4} reports the finetuned quality on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.
After sequential finetuning (13.5 GPU-hours, 26{,}231 steps), the available
single-run estimate is 0.46 versus 0.46 EM on hotpot\_qa for ddi\_qf and
full. Mixed-layout finetuning (13.8 GPU-hours, coin-flip per record between
sequential and restart positions) yields 0.39 versus 0.40, and restart-only
finetuning (13.8 GPU-hours, restart positions exclusively) yields 0.42 versus
0.39. The full-quality change
(0.57$\to$0.46$\to$0.40$\to$0.39) may reflect a trade-off between the two
attention layouts; the available single-run estimates cannot isolate its cause.

On musique, the 8-layer finetune leaves a residual gap (0.13 vs 0.23 EM,
$\Delta$=+0.10). Doubling the trainable layers to 16 (3.73B params,
17.97 GPU-hours, building on the 8L checkpoint) narrows the musique gap
to $\Delta$=+0.04 (0.19 vs 0.23 EM); the same one-lineage evaluation gives
0.45 versus 0.45 on hotpot (Table~\ref{tab:t4-16l}). The student perplexity
is 1.453 versus 1.446 for the teacher.

The full-causal baseline has three seed-selected 100-record splits
(Table~\ref{tab:t6-delta}). Their descriptive 95\% t-intervals are
[0.328, 0.659] (hotpot EM), [0.550, 0.754] (hotpot F1), [0.135, 0.359]
(musique EM), and [0.259, 0.444] (musique F1). These intervals describe only
the mean full-baseline score across those three splits; they are not
fixed-set, paired mask-comparison intervals and do not transfer to finetuned,
restart, or Llama rows. The 500-record 16L estimates are $+0.014$ on hotpot
and $+0.036$ on musique, without a comparison interval.

(Table~\ref{tab:t4_restart}). The original evaluator supplied restart

has a single-run estimate of 0.47 restart versus 0.45 sequential EM on the
100-record hotpot\_qa subset. The previous numerical defect comparison is not
retained because its two displayed cells did not come from a hash-backed

\paragraph{Ablation: \texttt{ddi\_strict}.}
The available single-run estimates for \texttt{ddi\_strict}, where only the
last query row bridges into the documents, are lower than \texttt{ddi\_qf} on
the base and 16L-tuned checkpoints: hotpot\_qa EM is 0.24 versus 0.31 (base)
and 0.39 versus 0.45 (tuned). These observations do not establish that the
full-bridge query is necessary across checkpoints or evaluation sets.
